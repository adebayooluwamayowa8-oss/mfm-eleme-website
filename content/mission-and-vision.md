## Our mission

As part of MFM Worldwide, we share the gospel of Jesus Christ, encourage a life of holiness and prayer, and help believers grow in their relationship with God. We seek to equip people through Scripture, worship and spiritual formation.

## Our vision

We desire to see lives transformed through faith in Christ and people equipped to pray, serve and share the gospel in their communities. MFM Worldwide places particular emphasis on revival, the work of the Holy Spirit and deliverance ministry.

## At MFM Eleme

Our local expression of that mission is to welcome our neighbours in Eleme into a community of worship, prayer and the Word.
